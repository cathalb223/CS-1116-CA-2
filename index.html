Ranch Roundup!

Instructions

  • Use arrow keys to move
  • Press E to lasso nearby cows
  • Bring them into the pen in the top right corner
  • Dont get hit by cows!
  • Capture all cows to complete the level
  • Beat 10 levels to win

Cheats

  • Press 'p' to turn on hitboxes (used for debugging)
  • Press 'q' for lots of health
  • Press 'm' to automatically win a level
